# Business Context

## Company Snapshot

- Company: Brasaland
- Sector: Restaurant chain (charcoal grill)
- Scale: 14 stores in Colombia and Florida (USA)
- Team size: ~115 employees
- Annual revenue: ~USD 6M

## Strategic Goal

Build Brasaland Digital capabilities to modernize operations without losing brand consistency in food quality and customer experience.

## Core Business Constraints

1. Multi-country operation (Colombia + USA).
2. Multi-currency reporting (COP + USD).
3. Need for operational consistency across all stores.
4. Low system integration baseline (many processes are manual).

## Priority Problem Areas

1. Store operations visibility and inventory ordering.
2. Procurement and supplier cost control.
3. Customer digital experience (website, loyalty, CRM).
4. HR workflows and onboarding automation.
5. Training distribution and quality standards.
6. Executive visibility and decision support.

## Product Direction

Deliver user-facing and internal tools that create measurable business impact:

1. Real-time dashboards and alerts.
2. Digital touchpoints for customers and employees.
3. Centralized backend services and data pipelines.
